# Goals

1. Twelve Core Techniques  
   Six pure Runic Path techniques, two Body/Runic techniques, two Essence/Runic techniques, and two Soul/Runic techniques.

2. 18 Minor Techniques  
   One technique for each non-major Ascension Path, giving the Runic Path broad cross-path compatibility.

3. Multiple Runic Physiques with Evolutions  
   Around ten to twelve physiques, ranging from simple runic affinity bodies to high-end evolved physiques like Runic Eyes, Inscribing Body, Etching Meridians, and Runic Perfection.

4. Multiple Runic Bloodlines  
   Around five or six bloodlines, each with a specific runic identity, such as qi flow, flame violence, heaven script, hell script, earth script, or hidden script.

5. 9 Runic Brushes  
   Three brushes each for Heaven, Hell, and Earth themed progression, with different effects on stability, power, cost, rune slots, backlash, and formula behaviour.

6. 100 Basic Runes  
   A hard limit of around one hundred basic runes, since too many more would probably cause overlaps in names, effects, grammar roles, and identity.

7. Flexible Rune Grammar System  
   A grammar system where runes are arranged by source, intent, form, and modifier roles, allowing players to create formulae rather than only selecting pre-authored spells.

8. Near Infinite Runic Sequences  
   Players should be able to create a huge number of valid formulae by combining runes, techniques, brushes, inscriptions, physiques, bloodlines, and realm scaling.

9. Runic Sequences Scaling Based on Realm  
   Higher Runic realms should improve formula power, range, duration, stability, rune slots, casting time, and suppression control.

10. Sequence Backlash and Instability System  
    Unstable or badly formed formulae should risk qi loss, backlash damage, failed casting, partial casting, confusion, or stranger future consequences.

11. Rune Mastery and Formula Refinement System  
    Repeated use of formulae should improve familiarity and mastery, potentially reducing cost, improving stability, increasing power, and allowing better suppression.

12. Rare Casting Paradigms  
    Late-game or extremely rare casting systems that still use universal runes, but change how those runes are expressed. Planned paradigms include Common Casting, Acheron Casting, Mesh Casting, and Dream Casting.

13. Runic Sight and Observation System  
    A system for observing runes in the world. More known runes, higher realms, better physiques, techniques, and tools should make it easier to observe and understand traces.

14. Rune Discovery Through World Interaction  
    Players should discover runes through blocks, entities, structures, items, biomes, residues, inscriptions, and other traces found naturally in the world.

15. Runic Codex and Formula Recording  
    A personal book that records known runes, observed rune progress, scrap lessons, discovered formulae, formula mastery, and eventually inscriptions or favourite formulae.

16. Ancient Tablets, Tomes, Scraps, and Inscriptions  
    World-found learning items that teach runes, formula families, lore, grammar rules, instability, suppression, inscriptions, paradigms, and hidden progression.

17. Runic Carving into the Body  
    Body carving through evolvable passive inscriptions, giving permanent or semi-permanent effects based on rune patterns carved into the cultivator.

18. Evolvable Passive Inscriptions  
    Inscriptions should have four or five tiers across the ten Runic realms, with higher tiers requiring more runes, better materials, higher realms, and possibly more risk.

19. External Runic Carving  
    Runic carving into weapons, armour, artifacts, tools, and other equipment, allowing formulae and rune patterns to modify item behaviour.

20. Talisman Integration  
    Talismans should act as temporary or consumable formula carriers, allowing stored formulae, emergency casting, traps, seals, barriers, or support effects.

21. Runic Pills and Elixirs  
    Pills and elixirs for mitigating backlash, improving rune learning, increasing observation rate, boosting formula stability, enhancing carving success, or temporarily improving casting.

22. Runic Herbs, Plants, and Crafting Materials  
    Materials used for pills, elixirs, brushes, seals, inscriptions, carving tools, talismans, artifacts, and higher-tier runic crafting.

23. Runic Artifacts and Relics  
    Special runic items such as brushes, seals, needles, carving knives, codices, tablets, ancient relics, and high-end tools with unique runic effects.

24. Runic Tribulations and Breakthrough Trials  
    Each Runic Path breakthrough could require initiation and a trial. Trials could be randomly selected from a pool of three or four types, scaling in difficulty with realm.

25. Runic Structures and World Generation  
    Runic shrines, ruins, tablet chambers, formation halls, heaven pavilions, hell pits, earth monoliths, dreaming archives, and other structures that teach through exploration.

26. Heaven, Hell, and Earth Themed Progression  
    A major thematic split for brushes, bloodlines, inscriptions, structures, techniques, and formula tendencies. Heaven leans toward clarity and restoration, Hell toward power and danger, Earth toward stability and endurance.

27. Inner and Outer Rune Master Specialisation  
    Inner Rune Masters focus on body carving, passive inscriptions, survival, healing, and self-effects. Outer Rune Masters focus on external formulae, range, fields, traps, projectiles, and world manipulation.

28. Datapack, Addon, and Long-Term Expansion Support  
    Datapack support for runes, sequences, inscriptions, techniques, physiques, bloodlines, brushes, and future systems, plus support for external rune registration, optional addon integrations, cross-path synergies, addon-specific content, and long-term expansion of rune categories, grammar rules, and formula complexity.


# Runic Path

## Core Idea

The Runic Path is a cultivation path based around discovering, understanding, recording, combining, casting, refining, and eventually embodying the hidden script of reality.

Runes are not just spell parts. They are fragments of the world's underlying language.

A beginner Rune Master learns to see traces in the world.
A practiced Rune Master learns to arrange those traces into formulae.
A powerful Rune Master learns to carve formulae into the body, soul, weapons, armour, talismans, and artifacts.
A legendary Rune Master may eventually stop using common casting entirely, and instead express runes through rare casting paradigms that distort how formulae exist.

The current v0.1 direction is focused on making the first five realms feel playable and content-rich before expanding into the higher realms.

---

## Realms

1. Trace Sensing
2. Rune Knowing
3. Script Visualization
4. Pattern Enlightenment
5. Rune Breath
6. Soul Inscription
7. Living Script
8. Dao Seed
9. Origin Spark
10. Infinite Script

### Current Release Scope

For now, the addon is focused around the first five Runic realms, since Ascension currently has five released major realms.

Realms 6-10 should remain in the design and lore, but be treated as sealed future progression.

### Realm Design Notes

Higher realms should unlock:

* More rune slots
* Longer casting windows
* Better formula insight
* Deeper rune observation
* More stable formula casting
* Stronger brush interactions
* Better inscription evolution
* More advanced techniques
* Access to deeper rune families and hidden meanings

The early realms should teach the basic language of runes.

The later realms should make the player feel like they are no longer just writing formulae, but becoming part of the script itself.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

> Observe rune → Learn rune → Record rune → Combine rune → Cast formula → Refine formula → Inscribe formula → Master script

The loop should start simple:

1. Find a tome, scrap, tablet, structure, mob, block, or trace.
2. Use Runic Sight or study items to observe runes.
3. Record known runes and discovered formulae in the Runic Codex.
4. Use the Runic Casting screen to arrange runes into formulae.
5. Cast formulae through common casting.
6. Deal with qi cost, stability, backlash, and mastery.
7. Use inscriptions, techniques, bloodlines, physiques, and brushes to improve the way formulae behave.
8. Eventually specialise into different Runic styles or rare casting paradigms.

---

# Runic Path Entry Conditions

Possible entry requirements:

* At least Major Realm 1 in any major Ascension path.
* A discovered rune source, tome, tablet, teacher, or first inscription.
* A Runic Codex or similar item.
* Maybe a "First Inscription" breakthrough ritual later.

For v0.1, entry should probably stay simple enough that players can start testing the path without too much grind.

Possible v0.1 entry:

* Player is already on an Ascension path.
* Player studies a Runic Tome, Runic Scrap, or uses a starter Runic item.
* Player unlocks the Runic Path and gains access to the Runic Codex / Runic Casting system.

# Types of Runes

Runes are divided into broad categories, but these categories should stay flexible so new families can be added later.

## Source Runes

Core runes found in the natural world.

Examples:

* Flame
* Water
* Wind
* Earth
* Wood
* Metal
* Lightning
* Frost
* Light
* Shadow
* Life
* Decay

Source runes describe what kind of power the formula begins from.

## Intent Runes

Runes that describe what the source wants to do.

Examples:

* Cut
* Heal
* Guard
* Bind
* Push
* Pull
* Mark
* Release
* Gather

Intent runes give the formula direction and purpose.

## Form Runes

Runes that describe the shape or vessel of a formula.

Examples:

* Bolt
* Line
* Wall
* Circle
* Sphere
* Pulse

Form runes decide how the formula manifests.

## Modifier Runes

Runes that adjust how a formula behaves.

Examples:

* Quicken
* Stabilise
* Heavy
* Violent
* Hidden
* Compress
* Pierce

Modifier runes can strengthen, weaken, stabilise, distort, or specialise a formula.

They should be powerful, but they should also increase the chance of instability if used carelessly.

# Rune Depth and Discovery

Runes should be split by depth.

Possible depth names:

* Surface Runes
* Deep Runes
* Hidden Runes
* Ancient Runes
* Origin Runes

A block, entity, item, biome, or structure may contain multiple traces.

Example:

* Stone block: Earth
* Deepslate: Earth + Heavy
* Ancient debris: Earth + Metal + Hidden
* Campfire: Flame + Release
* Glowstone: Light
* Wither rose: Decay + Life
* Enderman: Shadow + Hidden
* Lightning rod: Lightning + Gather

Higher realms should allow the player to see deeper traces.

This means the same world object can become more meaningful as the player grows stronger.

# Learning a Rune

## States of Learning

Unseen Rune
The player has no idea that the rune exists.

Glimpsed Rune
The player has seen the rune, but only has a weak understanding of it.

Observed Rune
The player can perceive the rune clearly, but may not be able to use it yet.

Known Rune
The player understands the rune well enough to use it in casting, skills, inscriptions, and formulae.

## How Players Learn

Players can learn runes through:

* Runic Tomes
* Fragmented Formula Tomes
* Ancient Tablets
* Runic Scraps
* Runic Sight
* World traces
* Entity traces
* Structure inscriptions
* Future teachers, relics, or breakthroughs

Tomes should be more direct.
Scraps should be more tutorial-like.
Runic Sight should make the world itself feel readable.

# Runic Sight

Runic Sight is the player's main observation skill.

It lets players inspect blocks, entities, and traces in the world to discover runes.

Runic Sight should grow with:

* Runic realm
* Number of known runes
* Relevant techniques
* Runic Eyes physique
* Certain bloodlines
* Codex progression
* Future observation inscriptions

## Current Direction

Runic Sight should:

* Find runes in blocks and entities.
* Show when traces are too deep.
* Increase observation progress.
* Turn glimpsed runes into observed or known runes over time.
* Reward players for exploring the world.
* Eventually reveal formula residues, hidden inscriptions, traps, and structures.

## Future Runic Sight Progression

0 known runes:
No passive sight. Player must learn through tomes, scraps, or obvious sources.

10 known runes:
Runic Sight becomes a core active skill.

25 known runes:
Common mob and entity runes become easier to observe.

40 known runes:
Runic Visualization may become a passive or semi-passive sight effect.

75+ known runes:
Complex formulae, traps, hidden inscriptions, and spell residues become visible.

# Runic Casting

## Common Casting

Common Casting is the universal runic casting system.

This is the system most Rune Masters use.

It is the foundation that most techniques, lineages, inscriptions, and formulae are built upon.

Common Casting uses ordered sequences of runes.

Example:

```text
Flame -> Line -> Cut -> Violent
```

This could become something like a violent flaming cutting line.

The current Runic Casting screen and formula interpreter are part of Common Casting.

## Flexible Formula Grammar

The goal is not to author every possible spell.

The goal is for the player to arrange known runes into formulae that follow grammar rules.

Authored sequences should exist, but only as:

* Tutorial formulae
* Famous named formulae
* Ancient inherited formulae
* Special exceptions
* Examples for the player
* Formulae with custom effects that the generic system cannot yet express

The main system should remain flexible.

## Casting Profile

A formula should be interpreted through:

* Source
* Intent
* Form
* Modifiers
* Realm
* Suppression
* Brush
* Technique
* Physique
* Bloodline
* Inscriptions
* Stability
* Backlash
* Mastery

The player should be encouraged to experiment, but bad grammar should still have consequences.

---

# Formula Stability and Backlash

Runic formulae should not always be safe.

A formula can fail because:

* Missing source or intent
* Too many sources
* Too many intents
* Too many forms
* Bad rune order
* Overuse of violent or unstable modifiers
* Realm too low
* Qi too low
* Formula too complex
* Brush mismatch
* Suppression mismatch
* Unstable technique or bloodline interaction

Failure should usually produce:

* Action bar warning
* Qi loss
* Backlash damage
* Confusion / nausea / disruption
* Formula collapse
* Possible partial effect for unstable formulae

## Backlash Design

Backlash should not just be punishment.

It should be a design space.

Some techniques or bloodlines may accept higher backlash for stronger formulae.
Some inscriptions may reduce backlash.
Some brushes may increase power but worsen instability.
Some future paradigms may make backlash stranger instead of simply stronger.

---

# Formula Mastery and Refinement

Repeated use of a formula should eventually improve it.

Possible grades:

* Unstable
* Familiar
* Stable
* Mastered
* Perfected

Mastery could affect:

* Qi cost
* Stability
* Damage
* Range
* Duration
* Backlash risk
* Cast time
* Codex display
* Suppression control

This should be optional for early releases, but long-term it fits the Runic Path extremely well.

The player should feel like a formula becomes part of their personal scripture over time.

# Casting Paradigms

Most Rune Masters use Common Casting.

Common Casting is universal, teachable, and compatible with most runes, techniques, inscriptions, and brushes.

However, extremely rare late-game techniques or inheritances may unlock alternative casting paradigms.

These paradigms still use the same universal runes, but the way those runes are expressed changes completely.

The idea is:

> The language stays the same, but different masters learn entirely different ways to speak it.

## Common Casting

The baseline system.

Runes are arranged into ordered sequences and interpreted according to normal runic grammar.

Common Casting is reliable, understandable, and widely compatible.

## Acheron Casting

A rare space-time based casting paradigm.

The runes are still universal, but every expression of a rune takes on a spatial or temporal flavour.

Acheron Casting may allow:

* Compressing the space a rune occupies in a sequence
* Expanding the space a rune occupies in a sequence
* Overlapping the same rune from different moments
* Delaying rune expression
* Echoing past formulae
* Splitting a formula across time
* Turning a small rune into a large spatial event
* Making a formula hit before it visually completes

Example ideas:

* A compressed Cut rune becomes a spatial rupture.
* An expanded Guard rune becomes a defensive zone.
* Three overlapping Flame runes from different moments become a temporal burn.
* A delayed Bind rune catches a target after they move.

Acheron Casting should be incredibly rare and difficult to master.

## Mesh Casting

A rare casting paradigm that abandons simple sequences.

Instead of arranging runes in a line, the player creates a Runic Mesh.

A mesh is an interlocking network of runes.

Example:

```text
      Guard
        |
Earth - Core - Circle
        |
      Heavy
```

Mesh Casting may allow:

* Multiple runes to support one another
* Conflicting runes to coexist if separated by structure
* A simple mesh to be reinforced by a second overlay mesh
* Defensive, ritual, field, trap, and formation-like formulae
* Long-lasting formula structures
* Complex layered effects

Mesh Casting should start simple, then become very powerful as the player gains more mesh nodes.

It should feel less like casting a sentence and more like building a living diagram.

## Dream Casting

A rare dream-state casting paradigm.

The runes exist within a dream rather than fully in physical reality.

Dream formulae may express:

* Object impermanence
* Randomisation
* Abstraction
* Symbolic interpretation
* Passing through blocks
* Unstable targeting
* Illusory duplicates
* Effects that partially ignore normal geometry
* Formulae that change based on context or perception

Example ideas:

* A Dream Wall may block enemies but allow allies to pass.
* A Dream Flame may burn an idea of a target rather than the target directly.
* A Dream Step may pass through a wall but arrive slightly misplaced.
* A Dream Mark may attach to a name, memory, or intention instead of a body.
* A Dream Heal may heal unpredictably, but sometimes overperform.

Dream Casting should feel beautiful, strange, and unreliable.

It should be capable of effects that Common Casting cannot easily reproduce, but should also carry risks.

An offensive and external formula technique.

* Runic Sight

# Skills

Runic skills should make rune knowledge matter outside the casting screen.

A player should not feel like runes only exist inside the Codex.

## Current v0.1 Skill Direction

### Runic Casting

Opens the common casting interface.

The player arranges known runes into formulae.

### Runic Sight

Observes runic traces in the world.

Used for discovery and progression.

### Script Sever

Offensive skill.

A general cutting script that uses Runic damage.

Future augment idea:

* Flame Sever
* Lightning Sever
* Shadow Sever
* Metal Sever

### Cinder Brand

Offensive skill.

Marks a target with burning script.

Future augment idea:

* Release rune causes detonation
* Violent rune increases damage and backlash
* Hidden rune delays the brand
* Decay rune changes the burn into rot

### Stone Ward

Sustained defensive skill.

Consumes qi while held.

Should root the player and create a defensive ward that pushes enemies and deflects projectiles.

### Water Mantle

Sustained defensive / support skill.

Consumes qi while held.

Should extinguish the player, heal over time, dampen projectiles, and reduce fall danger.

### Trace Step

Utility movement skill.

Steps along a wind-veiled trace.

Range should scale with Runic realm, and future rune augments can change how the step behaves.

Future augment idea:

* Wind increases range
* Shadow hides the trace
* Lightning adds speed or damage
* Dream-style effects may allow partial phasing later
